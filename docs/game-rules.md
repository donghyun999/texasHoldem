# Game Rules

## Scope

- Single-table Sit and Go only
- Minimum 2 players, maximum 6 players
- No rebuy, addon, spectator mode, or multi-table merge

## Core tournament rules

- Starting stack is 1000 chips for every ready player
- Blinds increase every 5 minutes and apply from the next hand
- Supported actions are `fold`, `check`, `call`, `bet`, `raise`, and `all-in`
- Short blind or short call situations become all-in instead of invalid actions
- Main pot and side pots are calculated on total contribution tiers
- The tournament ends immediately when one surviving player remains
